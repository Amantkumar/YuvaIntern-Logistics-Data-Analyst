## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains my complete **4-week Logistics Data Analyst Internship project at Yuva Intern**.

The internship focuses on applying data analytics and data science techniques to realistic logistics and supply-chain problems.

Across four weeks, the project follows an end-to-end analytical journey:

**Strategic Planning → Data Collection & Cleaning → Advanced Analysis & Visualization → Predictive Modeling & Optimization**

The overall objective is to understand logistics operations, identify important performance indicators, prepare and analyze logistics data, develop predictive models, and propose data-driven strategies for improving operational efficiency and resource allocation.

---

# 🎯 Internship Objectives

The major objectives of this internship are:

- Understand logistics and supply-chain analytics.
- Define realistic logistics business problems.
- Identify relevant Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Research and collect suitable logistics data.
- Clean and preprocess datasets using Python.
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Create meaningful data visualizations.
- Identify operational patterns and bottlenecks.
- Apply predictive modeling techniques.
- Explore logistics optimization strategies.
- Generate actionable business insights.
- Support data-driven decision-making.
